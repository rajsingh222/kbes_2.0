How to Create a Basic Assessment

Version 1.0 For end users & field engineers

Introduction

OSHMAS is an AI-powered system for assessing the structural health of buildings, bridges, tunnels and towers. A Basic Assessment is the starting point — it captures key details about a structure, processes a secure payment and then generates an AI-driven structural health report with a downloadable PDF.

This guide walks you through every step from login to downloading your completed report. The entire process typically takes 10–15 minutes.

📌 Before you begin Have the following information ready: structure name/address, year of construction, location, current usage type and your payment card details.

Register / Log In

You need an OSHMAS account before you can run an assessment. New users must register; returning users can log in directly.

New User — Sign Up

  1. Open the OSHMAS application URL in your browser.
  2. Click "Sign Up" or "Register" on the home screen.
  3. Enter your Full Name, Email Address and Password.
  4. Click "Create Account" and verify your email via the link sent to your inbox.

3

Enter Your Personal / Organization Details

The first section of the form collects your contact and organization information. This personalizes the report and determines where it is emailed.

  1. Enter your Full Name (or the name of the responsible engineer).
  2. Enter your Email Address — this is where the completed report will be delivered.
  3. Enter your Phone Number.
  4. Enter your Organization / Company name.
  5. Double-check all fields and click "Next".

5

Fill in the Assessment Form

This section collects fundamental information about the structure. You will be asked about the structure's name or location, its year of construction, current usage / occupancy type and the environmental exposure conditions it is subjected to (normal, coastal, industrial, etc.).

  1. Fill in every field in this section as accurately as possible.
  2. For drop-down fields, choose the option that best matches the structure's actual condition.
  3. Click "Next" to move to the detailed diagnostic questions.

6

Answer Structural Distress Questions

This is the core diagnostic section of the assessment. The questions are automatically tailored to the structure type you selected in Step 4. They ask about visible signs of distress — such as cracks, moisture, corrosion, foundation settlement, and past disaster exposure — observed in the structure.

For each question, select the severity level or condition that best matches what you have physically observed. The accuracy of your answers directly influences the quality of the AI-generated report.

  1. Read each question and its options carefully.
  2. Select the answer that most accurately reflects the current observed condition.
  3. If you are unsure about a specific item, select the closest available option.
  4. Answer all questions on this screen, then click "Next".

Review & Confirm Your Answers

  2. If anything needs to be changed, click the "Edit" button next to that section.
  3. Once all answers are correct, click "Proceed to Payment".

9

View & Download Your Report

After payment is verified, OSHMAS automatically generates your structural health report. This process takes approximately 15–45 seconds.

⚙️ What OSHMAS does automatically after payment

  • Sends your assessment responses to the AI engine (Groq AI)
  • Generates a comprehensive 4,000–5,000 word structural health report
  • Creates a professionally formatted PDF report
  • Stores the PDF securely in the cloud
  • Emails the PDF to your registered email address
  1. Wait for the report generation screen to complete (a progress indicator will be shown).
  2. Once ready, you will be taken to the Assessment Results page.
  3. Click "View Report" to read the full report on screen.
  4. Click "Download PDF" to save the report to your device.
  5. Check your email inbox — a copy of the PDF has also been sent automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I save my assessment midway and continue later?

Yes. OSHMAS auto-saves your progress. Return to it via My Assessments → Drafts on the dashboard.

What if I selected the wrong structure type?

Use the "Back" button before submission to correct it. After payment and submission you will need to start a new assessment.

My payment was deducted but I did not receive the report. What do I do?

Do not pay again. Go to My Assessments on the dashboard — your assessment will be listed there. If the report is still not generated, contact your system administrator with your payment ID.

I did not receive the report email.

Check your spam/junk folder. If not found, go to the Assessment Results page and click "Resend Email". You can also download the PDF directly from the results page.

How accurate is the AI-generated report?

The report is generated using advanced AI trained on structural engineering knowledge and provides expert-level observations. It should be treated as a preliminary assessment tool and verified by a qualified structural engineer for critical decisions.

Can I do an advanced assessment after completing a basic one?

Yes. Once your Basic Assessment is complete, you can initiate an Advanced Assessment from the Assessment Results page for a deeper in-depth analysis.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted. PDF reports are stored on secured cloud infrastructure accessible only through your account.
